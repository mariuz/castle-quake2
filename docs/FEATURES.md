# Features

Everything the port does, with the Castle Game Engine (CGE) parts each feature uses. For how the pieces fit together, see [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md); for what is still missing compared with Quake 2, see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

Random levels and the level editor have their own page: [LEVELS.md](LEVELS.md).

- **Quake 2 BSP (IBSP v38) world geometry**
  - All 19 lumps parsed; faces batched per texture + surface flags into X3D `TIndexedTriangleSetNode`s loaded into `TCastleScene` with `PreciseCollisions` for walking and ray casts.
  - **WAL wall textures** decoded with the `pics/colormap.pcx` palette and served through the `quaketex:` URL protocol.
  - **Baked RGB lightmaps** packed into one atlas per model and applied with `TMultiTextureNode` (`MODULATE` × 2, the overbright factor in the unlit material's emissive colour, which also works in WebGL), like Quake 2's GL renderer.
  - Surface flags: `SURF_SKY` (invisible but solid), `SURF_NODRAW`, `SURF_WARP` (animated with a GLSL `PLUG_texture_coord_shift` shader effect, non-solid), `SURF_TRANS33/66` (blended), `SURF_FLOWING` (scrolling `TTextureTransformNode`).
  - **Animated textures** following the WAL `animname` chains at 2 frames/second.
  - **Light styles**: flickering, pulsing and switchable lights. Faces lit by animated styles live in a separate lightmap atlas that is recomposed from their up to 4 style layers (10 Hz, only when a style changes) and re-uploaded; `light` entities with a `targetname` switch on/off, `target_lightramp` fades them.
- **Skybox** from `env/<sky>{rt,lf,up,dn,bk,ft}.tga|pcx` via `TCastleBackground` (`TQuakeMap.Background`, also shown in the CGE editor).
- **SP2 sprites** (IDS2 v2): the BFG10K ball, its explosion and the translucent flash on what the BFG effect hits (`s_bfg1` / `s_bfg3` / `s_bfg2`), as animated camera-facing quads. The demo PAK's other sprites (`s_bubble`, `s_flash`, `s_explo*`) aren't used by Quake 2's code either.
- **MD2 models** (IDP2 v8): monsters, items, weapons, projectiles and the first-person view weapon, with **interpolated frame animation**, PCX skins (index 255 transparent) and per-model tint sampled from the lightmaps below them (Quake 2's `R_LightPoint`, done by walking the BSP nodes). **Shells** like `RF_SHELL_*`: a translucent copy pushed 4 units out along the normals, blue on the view weapon with quad damage, red with invulnerability, green on monsters whose power armor absorbs a hit (the brain's power screen).
- **Brush entities** as `TCastleTransform`s: `func_door`, `func_door_rotating`, `func_plat`, `func_button`, `func_rotating`, `func_train` (follows `path_corner`s, stops at `wait -1` corners; multi-floor elevators with `trigger_elevator`: each button's `pathtarget` names the floor), `func_wall`, `func_explosive` (shootable, chain reactions).
- **Quake 2 entity logic**: `target` / `targetname` chains (`G_UseTargets`), `trigger_multiple/once/relay/counter/key/hurt/always`, `delay`, `target_changelevel` (with `map$spawnpoint`; levels of a unit remember their state when you come back), `target_crosslevel_trigger` / `_target`, `target_secret/goal/help/speaker/explosion`, `misc_teleporter`, messages and the help-computer objectives.
- **Dynamic lights**: muzzle flashes, explosions, rockets, blaster bolts and BFG balls light the walls and models around them (a shader effect on the lightmaps).
- **All 10 weapons plus hand grenades**: Blaster, Shotgun, Super Shotgun, Machinegun, Chaingun (spins up from 1 to 3 bullets per frame), Grenade Launcher (bouncing grenades), Rocket Launcher (splash damage), HyperBlaster, Railgun (piercing, spiral trail), BFG10K (in flight, green lasers hit monsters within 256 units for 10 damage every 0.1 s; after the impact, the BFG effect hits everything in sight of the blast and the shooter within 1000 units for up to 500, like `bfg_think` / `bfg_explode`), hand grenades (hold fire to cook, throw harder the longer you hold, explode in your hand after 3 s). Guns run on Quake 2's weapon frames: shots leave on the frames the original fires them, refire when the animation allows, and switching weapons plays the put-away and raise animations (the BFG fires its ball 0.8 s after the flash, like the original). Shots are Quake 2 traces through the map's brushes, doors and plats, monster boxes (shorter while a monster ducks), corpses and barrels; grenades bounce off the surface they hit.
- **Items**: health, armor (jacket/combat/body with Quake 2 protection and salvage rules, shards), ammo, quad damage, invulnerability, adrenaline, bandolier, ammo pack, rebreather and environment suit (30 s of air underwater; the suit also takes lava down to a third and stops slime damage, with Quake 2's green tints and `airout` warning), silencer (30 shots monsters don't hear, quieter muzzle sounds), power screen / shield (absorb a third / two thirds of each hit for cells, the screen only from the front; the HUD's armor slot shows the cells; on with the first one, `powerarmor` switches it; Quake 2 single player keeps these items in an inventory until used, here they're used when picked up), keys (data CD, power cube, pyramid, spinner, pass, blue/red key, commander's head, airstrike marker).
- **Monsters** (22 types defined; the demo has soldiers, enforcers, gunners, berserkers, flyers, parasites and tanks; with the full game, the gladiator, iron maiden, mutant, brain, technician (floater), icarus (hover), barracuda shark (flipper), medic, supertank, hornet, Jorg and the Makron attack and move on their Quake 2 frames too: the gladiator's railgun aimed where you were, the iron maiden's rocket refires, the mutant's leap, the brain's pulling tentacles, the hornet's rocket volleys, Jorg's twin chainguns and BFG on Jorg's legs, the Makron's sweeping hyperblaster, launched from Jorg when it dies): sight via line-of-sight checks, chase at their Quake 2 pace (walk / run frames moving the original per-frame distances), floor following, melee / hitscan / blaster / rocket / grenade / railgun attacks, pain and death animations, gibbing into flying gib models; monsters detour around obstacles and won't walk off ledges; idle monsters walk their `path_corner` patrols (with `wait` and `pathtarget`), alerted ones first run to their `point_combat` (and hold it when flagged); monster projectiles can hurt other monsters.
- **View weapon in its own transparent viewport** (`WeaponViewport`, cleared depth), so it never clips into walls.
- **Invisible player-clip walls** rebuilt from the clip brushes' planes.
- **Player physics ported from Quake 2's pmove** (`TQuakePlayerMovement`, a behavior on the camera): Quake 2's 32 × 32 × 56 player box (32 high when crouched) swept through the map's BSP brushes like Quake 2's collision model (`QuakeTrace`: exact sliding along walls, slopes and creases, 18-unit step-ups with a smoothed view, platforms and doors carry and push you, monsters block you), friction 6, accelerate 10, air control 1, gravity 800, jump 270, 18-unit steps, crouch (eye 46 → 22, speed 100), ladders (forward + look up / down, jump / crouch), falling damage and jump pads (`trigger_push`). Tunables are published, so they can be tweaked in the editor; `Active = false` falls back to `TCastleWalkNavigation` movement. **Swimming** (`PM_WaterMove`: water level from BSP leaf contents, water friction, sinking, swim up / along the view), underwater tints and view warp (the software renderer's `D_WarpScreen` as a screen effect), lava / slime damage, drowning after 12 seconds, splash sounds; warped liquid surfaces don't collide. Plus noclip, damage with armor absorption, death and level restart.
- **HUD** drawn with the original `pics/*.pcx` big numbers, ammo/health/armor/weapon/key icons and crosshair (`TDrawableImage`), pickup notices and damage / quad / invulnerability screen blends.
- **Spatial audio** from the PAK's WAVs via `TCastleSoundSource` (doors and lifts loop their move sound, idle monsters mutter), with Quake 2's distance attenuation mapped to OpenAL's linear model; **ambient `target_speaker` loops** (80–90 per demo map) and triggered sounds; optional CD-music replacement as `data/music/trackNN.ogg`.
- **Turrets** (`turret_breach`, `turret_base`, `turret_driver`): the gun turns to where its infantry driver aims and fires rockets; kill the driver to stop it.
- **Props and effects**: exploding barrels (`misc_explobox`, chain reactions), waving banners, corpses, the satellite dish, flying Strogg ships along their `path_corner`s, `func_timer`, `target_splash` sparks, `target_laser` beams, Quake II Remastered's `dynamic_light` (a run-time light with its radius, colour, intensity and light style, switched by its targetname), and Quake 2's animated, skin-fading explosion model.
- **Help computer (F1)** with the original `help.pcx`, level name, primary / secondary objectives and kills / goals / secrets, and an **intermission** at `info_player_intermission` before the next unit loads.
- **Particles** batched into one scene (camera-facing quads with per-vertex colour / alpha and a soft round sprite): bullet impacts, blood, blaster sparks, splashes, rail trails, BFG blasts, teleport fountains.
- **Savegames** (F6 / F9 quick save / load, `save` / `load <name>` in the console): the whole level state as JSON in the user config directory (`castle-config:/saves/`) — player, inventory, monsters, items, doors / plats / trains, barrels, ships, triggers, counters, timers, lasers, speakers, switchable lights, objectives.
- Drop-down **console**, **menu** with the map list read from the loaded PAKs, and a headless **autotest / demo-script harness**.
